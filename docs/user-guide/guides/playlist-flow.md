# See the flow of a practice playlist

Open an ordinary playlist, then choose **Playlist flow** above the track table.
Choose it again to return to the table.

![Saved playlist order with whole-track energy and adjacent BPM/key comparisons](../assets/screens/playlist-flow.png)

The overview follows saved playlist order even if the table was sorted by
another column. Each row shows whole-track energy, BPM and the change from the
previous track, plus adjacent harmonic key compatibility. These are preparation
aids: listen to the recordings before deciding on a transition.

Select a track to highlight it, or choose its preview button to hear it in the
persistent player. Use the overview's previous/next buttons to browse its loaded
rows. **Load more track details** fetches more of a larger playlist.

Unknown measurements remain unknown. The overview does not compare across a
track whose details are not loaded; a partial overview states its loaded count.
Hidden or unavailable playlist members may leave gaps. It does not calculate
transition lengths, overlap duration or per-segment energy.
