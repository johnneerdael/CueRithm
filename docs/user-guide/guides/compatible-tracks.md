# Find compatible tracks

Open **Compatible tracks** from the library, then choose a prepared source track. Search by artist or title if needed. Click **Find compatible tracks** to preview candidates from Tracks. Incoming and archived tracks are excluded, as is the source itself.

The first search uses your saved harmonic/fuzzy key preference and a BPM tolerance of ±4 BPM, including the boundary. Change the key mode for this search without changing your library preference. Disable key or BPM matching to search without that measurement.

Enable **Include half/double BPM** to also compare each candidate at half and double its BPM. The result explains the comparison used; a 64 BPM track can match a 128 BPM source when compared at ×2. This does not change the track's saved BPM.

Optionally require whole-track energy within a chosen tolerance, an exact genre value (ignoring case), or every selected custom tag. Energy matching starts at ±1 when enabled. Unknown key, BPM and energy cannot match an enabled check. If your source lacks a required measurement, prepare or correct it, or disable that check.

Results show the actual conditions that matched. Exact keys come first, followed by smaller BPM differences, optional energy differences, artist, title and a stable internal tie-breaker. Matching is a preparation aid. Preview tracks through the persistent player and listen before choosing a transition.

Select results, enter a playlist name and click **Save selected to playlist**. CueRithm creates an ordinary playlist in result order, with one Undo action in the sidebar. It is a snapshot and does not update automatically. Changing matching options or library measurements clears the preview so you can search again.
